## What is an Independent Clause?

An **independent clause** is a clause that expresses a complete thought and can stand alone as a fully grammatical sentence. Because it doesn't rely on any other part of the sentence to make sense, it represents the main idea.

### Characteristics

1. **Has a subject and a verb:** Like all clauses, it contains the core elements of an action and an actor.
2. **Expresses a complete thought:** It leaves no grammatical suspense.

### Examples

- "The cat slept on the mat."
- "I enjoy reading books."
- "She will travel to Japan next year."

Notice how each of these could be punctuated with a period and read perfectly fine on their own.

### Role in Complex Sentences

Independent clauses act as the main anchor of a sentence. When connected with conjunctions like *and*, *but*, or *so*, multiple independent clauses can form compound sentences.
