## What is the Past Simple?

The **past simple** is a verb tense used to talk about a completed action in a time before now.

### Form
For regular verbs, you add *-ed* to the base form. Irregular verbs have unique past forms that must be memorized.

### Examples
- "She **worked** late yesterday." *(Regular)*
- "I **went** to the store." *(Irregular)*
- "They **lived** in London for five years." *(Completed duration)*
