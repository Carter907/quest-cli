## What are Modal Verbs?

**Modal verbs** are auxiliary (helper) verbs used to express ability, permission, obligation, or possibility. They change the "mode" or attitude of the main verb.

### Form
Modals never change form (they do not take an *-s* or *-ed*) and are always followed by the base form of the main verb without "to".

### Examples
- "**Will**" (Certainty: *I will go*)
- "**Would**" (Hypothetical: *I would go*)
- "**Can**" (Ability: *I can go*)
- "**Might**" (Possibility: *I might go*)
