## What is a Past Participle?

The **past participle** is a specific verb form used to construct perfect tenses and passive voice sentences. It is not a tense on its own, but a grammatical building block.

### Form
For regular verbs, the past participle is identical to the past simple (it ends in *-ed*). For irregular verbs, it can end in *-en*, *-n*, *-t*, or remain unchanged.

### Examples
- "Walked" (Regular: *walk*)
- "Eaten" (Irregular: *eat*)
- "Broken" (Irregular: *break*)
