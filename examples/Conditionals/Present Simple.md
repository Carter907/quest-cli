## What is the Present Simple?

The **present simple** is a verb tense used to describe habitual actions, general facts, and unchanging situations.

### Form
For most verbs, you use the base form. For the third-person singular (he/she/it), you add an *-s* or *-es*.

### Examples
- "Water **boils** at 100 degrees Celsius." *(Fact)*
- "I **walk** to work every day." *(Habit)*
- "The earth **orbits** the sun." *(General truth)*
