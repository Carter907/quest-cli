## What is a Clause?

In English grammar, a **clause** is a structural unit that contains, at minimum, a **subject** and a **verb**. It represents a single, cohesive piece of information or action within a sentence.

### The Basic Components

- **Subject:** The person, place, thing, or idea that the clause is about or that performs the action.
- **Verb:** The action or state of being expressed in the clause.

### Examples

- "The sun shines."
  - **Subject:** The sun
  - **Verb:** shines
  
- "Because she was tired..."
  - **Subject:** she
  - **Verb:** was tired

While all clauses have a subject and a verb, not all clauses form complete sentences on their own. They serve as the foundational building blocks for forming more complex thoughts in language.
