## The First Conditional

The **first conditional** is used to talk about real, highly likely, or very possible situations in the future. It describes a specific condition and its probable future result.

### Structure

The condition is framed in the present tense, while the result is projected into the future using a modal verb (usually *will*).

- **If Clause:** Present Simple
- **Main Clause:** *Will* + Base Verb

### Examples

- "If it rains tomorrow, we will cancel the picnic."
- "If I study hard, I will pass the exam."
- "She will miss the bus if she doesn't hurry."

### Meaning

Unlike the zero conditional which deals in absolute facts, the first conditional deals in realistic predictions. The condition hasn't happened yet, but there is a strong possibility that it will.
