## What is the Past Perfect?

The **past perfect** is a verb tense used to make it clear that one specific event happened before another event in the past. 

### Form
It is formed using the past simple of the auxiliary verb *to have* (**had**) combined with the **past participle** of the main verb.

### Examples
- "I **had eaten** breakfast before I left the house."
- "She realized she **had forgotten** her keys."
